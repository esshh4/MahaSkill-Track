MahaSkillTrack

Skill Training Outcome Intelligence

Track Maharashtra skill-development outcomes from training to employment: placement, retention, salary growth and skill gaps in one platform.

Key Features

Track

Follow every trainee from enrollment and certification through to employment, self-employment or apprenticeship.

Measure

Measure placement, 3/6/12-month retention, salary growth and satisfaction with verified follow-up data.

Analyze

Identify skill gaps and course-provider performance with district-level analytics and multi-year cohort views.

Verify

Ensure data integrity with role-based access, telephonic follow-ups, and employer verification workflows.

Report

Generate reports on placement, retention and salary outcomes with exportable data for program review.

Comply

Respect trainee consent preferences and deliver only privacy-safe, anonymised analytics and insights.
